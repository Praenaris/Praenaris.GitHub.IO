This privacy notice applies to this website and to all games published by Praenaris (“we”, “us”), on every platform where they are available.


## Who we are

Praenaris is a videogame and software development company based in Donostia / San Sebastián, Basque Country, Spain. We are responsible for the personal information described in this notice, and you can reach us at any time at [contact@praenaris.com](mailto:contact@praenaris.com).


## What data we collect

We do not collect, store or share any personal information through our games. All of our games are currently single-player, and have no accounts, no ads and no in-game purchases.

This website does not use cookies, analytics or any kind of tracking.

If you write to us by email, we receive your email address and whatever information you include in your message. We use it only to answer you, keep it no longer than needed for that purpose, and never share it with anyone.


## What stays on your device

Your settings and game progress are saved only on your device, and we never receive them.


## Third-party services

Our games are mainly made with the Unity engine, which may send technical information about your device, such as its model, operating system or crash reports, to Unity Technologies. See [Unity’s privacy policy](https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy).

The Steam versions of our games use Steam features such as achievements, which are handled by Valve under the [Steam privacy policy](https://store.steampowered.com/privacy_agreement/).

Stores such as [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/privacy_agreement/), [itch.io](https://itch.io/docs/legal/privacy-policy) and the [PlayStation Store](https://www.playstation.com/legal/privacy-policy/) handle downloads and payments under their own privacy policies. We do not receive your payment details.

This website is hosted on [GitLab Pages](https://about.gitlab.com/privacy/), which may log your IP address when you visit it. It also loads its fonts from [Google Fonts](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and part of its styles from [Cloudflare](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/), so your browser connects to their servers and shares your IP address with them.


## Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the right to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, to restrict or object to its use, and to receive a copy of it. To exercise any of these rights, write to us at [contact@praenaris.com](mailto:contact@praenaris.com).

If you believe we have not handled your information properly, you can also file a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency ([AEPD](https://www.aepd.es/)).


## Children

We do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.


## Changes

We will publish any change to this notice on this page and update the date above.
