About us

Into deep waters

Our history

Praenaris began in late 2020, when a group of students got together to create their first videogame, originally entitled Project Circenses. This project would grow into The Silent Swan, the company’s first large videogame, released to critical acclaim in November 2023.

On the way there, Arima Lodge was born, a free-to-play game for PC, released on itch.io in September 2022, and reworked and released on Steam in late 2024.

Right around the same time in late 2022, Project Umbra began development. The project, whose official title is Embers of the Evening, was a first-person psychological horror videogame cancelled in mid-2024.

Currently, the team is working on the Nemo series of projects. The first of those projects, an illustrated short story entitled Chasing the Sun, released for free in late 2025.

Our ship, the Praenaris

The name of our company and ship, the Praenaris, comes from the words “praenatare”, to sail or swim in Latin, and “Polaris”, the northern star. We imagine our everyday work and effort to be similar to that of an icebreaker sailing across the arctic oceans in search of the northern star. The star, in turn, represents our desire and willingness to be a part of the modern technological era creating lasting works in both videogames and software.

Thus, the Praenaris, both in name and concept, embodies our commitment to face the odds, sail across the storms, and open up a path there where there is seemingly none.
